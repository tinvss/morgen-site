Support & contact

Morgen is a private journal for adults who want to reflect on their drinking habits. Find help with the iPhone app and Morgen Plus here.

Set a reminder for a particular day

Open Settings → For you → Reminder and allow notifications. Under Repeat, choose a weekday or Daily. Open Time, set the time and tap Save time. In Settings you can preview the Lock Screen text below the time and use the same switch to turn reminders off.

Export your data

In Morgen, open Settings → Your data → Export local data. Choose where to send or save the file in the iPhone share sheet. Morgen removes its temporary export file when the share sheet closes. Copies you save or send outside the app must be managed at their destination.

Delete local data

Open Settings → Your data → Delete all local data and confirm. This removes your profile, journal, intentions, weekly plans, workshop answers, situation cards, exports and local statistics identifier from this iPhone, and turns statistics off. A purchase stays linked to your Apple account. It does not remove exports saved elsewhere, system backups or purchase records.

Restore purchases

Open Settings → Morgen Plus → Restore purchases and use the same Apple account as for the purchase. Restore checks your purchase access. Restoring a purchase does not load your journal entries. Your device backup may include your journal, depending on your settings. If no purchase is found, check which Apple account you used and your purchase history with Apple.

Request a refund from Apple

For purchases billed by Apple, you can ask Apple for a refund. Apple reviews your request. Apple: Request a refund for apps or content.

Contact
