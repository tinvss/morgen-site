Privacy notice

Controller. Viral Vision LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA, represented by Tinus Klauser. Contact us about privacy at morgen@joinviralvision.de.

Your entries. Morgen stores your reason and everyday situation, daily intentions, weekly plans, daily reflections with your drink count, mood and notes, and weekly observations on your iPhone. Reading notes, workshop drafts, results and progress, and situation cards also stay in protected app storage. Patterns are calculated on the device over the last 28 calendar days. The moment helper discards unsaved choices when closed. Confirming an intention saves your choice for that day.

Morgen processes drinking records and other journal entries on your iPhone. It does not link them to purchase or analytics identifiers. There is no Morgen account or cloud sync provided by Morgen. Using these features is voluntary. Your own writing may reveal information about your health or habits. We have no journal server and cannot view or restore your journal for you. Local reminders are scheduled only after you grant system permission. Their text contains no journal content. Your iPhone settings determine whether device backups include app data.

Morgen Plus. When you open the app, Morgen uses RevenueCat to check available offers and your purchase access. RevenueCat assigns an app user identifier and processes technical details such as the app version. When you buy or restore a purchase, the product identifier and purchase history are processed so the app can grant the correct access. RevenueCat also uses purchase data for the provider’s purchase reports. When available, the SDK also sends the iOS identifier for vendor (IDFV) to validate purchase receipts. This identifier may appear in RevenueCat’s operational request logs for monitoring and troubleshooting. According to RevenueCat, these request logs are retained for 60 days. Purchase processing is independent of optional usage statistics. Apple processes your App Store payment. Morgen does not receive your payment details. The app does not send your journal, workshop answers or cards to Apple for payment processing or to RevenueCat. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

Usage statistics. PostHog EU, the service intended for optional usage statistics, is inactive in this version. The app does not create an analytics identifier, send events to PostHog or show a statistics switch. Morgen shows no advertising and does not use your entries to build advertising profiles. Any previously saved consent is reset while this service is unavailable. If a later version offers optional usage statistics, you can make a new choice based on the notices provided then.

Export and deletion. You can edit and delete individual entries or export a file. You choose where to send it in the iPhone share sheet. The app removes its temporary files after sharing, on next launch or on local erasure. Copies you sent elsewhere and system backups must be managed at their destination. Local content stays until you delete it. Erasing all local data removes workshop answers and other app records. The Store handles purchase entitlements. Erasing your journal leaves your purchase history with Apple and RevenueCat intact.

Visiting the website. Netlify serves the Morgen website. When you visit, Netlify processes technical request data such as your IP address, requested page, time and response status to deliver the site, identify technical problems and detect misuse. Our pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising trackers or contact forms.

Emailing support. If you write to us, we process your sender address, message and any attachments you include. Our support email runs through Google Workspace on the company domain joinviralvision.de. Incoming messages are forwarded to a monitored private mailbox for a response. That mailbox and its email provider receive the forwarded message. We do not need journal extracts for technical support. You decide what to share.

Purposes and legal bases. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on Article 6(1)(b) for processing needed to complete purchases, grant or restore access, and respond to requests about your contract. For other enquiries, our legitimate interest is to be able to respond. Other legitimate interests are keeping the website and purchase functions secure and reliable, and reviewing purchases in our sales reports (Article 6(1)(f)). Where applicable law requires us to retain records, we rely on Article 6(1)(c). Apple operates its store under its own terms.

Retention and international processing. For support messages, the relevant criteria are the time needed to handle your request and follow-up questions and any applicable legal recordkeeping duties. Purchase records are needed to verify and restore your one-time unlock and meet applicable recordkeeping duties. The relevant criteria for technical website logs are their operational and security purposes. Our company is based in the United States. Hosting, email and purchase processing may also involve processing outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. You can ask for details of recipients and the safeguards applicable to your data using the contact above.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, or object to certain processing. You may object on grounds relating to your particular situation where processing is based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. You may complain to your data protection authority. Contact us to discuss what we can link to a pseudonymous identifier. You can access and export your device journal yourself. Pattern calculations do not determine eligibility, rights or benefits.
